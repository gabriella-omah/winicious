Privacy Policy

Last updated · 1 September 2026

This policy explains how Winicious Hair and Beauty collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit our website, book services, or shop with us.

1. Who we are

Winicious is a hair and beauty salon based in Ilasan, Lekki, Lagos, Nigeria. We offer in-salon services and sell hair, tools, and beauty products through our website.

2. Information we collect

We may collect:

  • Identity & contact details — name, phone number, email, and delivery address.
  • Booking details — services, appointment dates, and notes you provide.
  • Order details — products purchased and order history.
  • Payment-related information — handled by our payment providers; we do not store full card details on our servers.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited.
  • Communications — messages via forms, WhatsApp, email, or social media.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Process bookings and product orders
  • Confirm appointments and deliveries
  • Respond to enquiries and provide support
  • Improve our website, services, and products
  • Send important updates about orders or bookings
  • Send marketing only where you have opted in
  • Meet legal and regulatory obligations

4. Sharing & disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with:

  • Payment processors, to complete checkout
  • Delivery partners, to fulfil orders
  • IT and hosting providers who support our website
  • Professional advisers or authorities where required by law

Third parties are expected to handle your data securely and only for the purpose we specify.

5. Payments

Online payments are processed by secure third-party providers. Card and banking details are entered on their platforms. Winicious does not store complete payment card numbers on our systems.

6. Cookies & local storage

We may use cookies or local storage to keep the site working (for example, your shopping bag) and to understand site usage. You can control cookies in your browser. Disabling some may affect features such as the cart.

7. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, including bookings, orders, and legal requirements. When no longer required, we delete or anonymise it where reasonably possible.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable Nigerian law, you may request to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete information where we no longer need it
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications

Contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity first.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No internet transmission is fully secure; please protect your devices and accounts.

10. Children

Our website and shop are not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect their personal data. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information so we can delete it.

11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the revised policy.
